catch_valley_ball — expert controller demos (push custom catch box)

default  : wall_bounce_enabled=false, enable_distractor=false
           red ball rolls straight to the exit edge (no wall rebound)
opt1     : wall_bounce_enabled=true,  enable_distractor=false
           red ball rebounds off a side rail mid-run
opt2     : wall_bounce_enabled=false, enable_distractor=true
           black distractor on a separate randomized lane (exit sep ≥ 1 diameter)
opt1+2   : wall_bounce_enabled=true,  enable_distractor=true
           wall rebound + black distractor (ball–ball bounce on contact)

Success:
  - red ball ends in the pushed open box beyond the red line (PhysX flight from
    ramp exit; no teleport into the box), AND
  - arm never touches the red ball, AND
  - black distractor (if present) is NOT in the box.

The catcher is a procedural open box (not a grasped cup). The expert contact-pushes
it from below the platform's lower Y edge to the landing; the box only moves under
gripper contact (no end teleport). Ball motion starts concurrently with the push.

Layout mirror disabled for these demos (right-arm / +x exit).
