default  : blocker_enabled=false, blocker_dynamic=false  (moving target only)
opt1     : blocker_enabled=true,  blocker_dynamic=false  (green static blocker)
opt2     : blocker_enabled=false, blocker_dynamic=true   (red moving blocker)
opt1+2   : blocker_enabled=true,  blocker_dynamic=true   (green static + red dynamic;
           red sits dual_blocker_gap=1cm in front of green)

Blocker–target gap (per episode, Uniform):
  Opt1 / Opt2 : blocker is sampled 5–8 cm in front of the target
                (blocker_y_gap ~ U[0.050, 0.080]).
  Opt1+2      : static (green) uses the same 5–8 cm sample; dynamic (red)
                stays dual_blocker_gap=0.010 m further in front of green.

Motion: target (and dynamic blocker) sway about table center x=0.
Speeds: target_speed / blocker_speed sampled per-ep as base ± speed_jitter_frac (20%).
Stick: spawns left or right; matching arm grasps and strikes.

Blockers: solid collision (no pass-through). Blocker disc centers are at the same
  height as the target / yellow bullseye (z-aligned; blocker_z_offset forced to 0).
  Stick–blocker contact fails the episode.
  Expert clears discs laterally (short dart tip→butt < gap_min) and plants short of
  the board face so the stick is not knocked out of the gripper.

Success:
  - tip contacts the yellow center (plane ≤ STICK_DIST=0.035 m AND
    radial offset ≤ center_radius=0.018 m), AND
  - stick never contacts a blocker (_hit_blocker must stay false).
