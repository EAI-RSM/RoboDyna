stop_valley_ball - expert controller demos

default  : wall_bounce_enabled=false, enable_distractor=false
           red ball rolls straight; bat held mid-air to stop it
opt1     : wall_bounce_enabled=true,  enable_distractor=false
           red ball rebounds off a side rail mid-run
opt2     : wall_bounce_enabled=false, enable_distractor=true
           black distractor on a separate lane (hitting it fails)
opt1+2   : wall_bounce_enabled=true,  enable_distractor=true
           wall rebound + black distractor

Success:
  - red ball contacts the red circular bat head (handle does not count), AND
  - ball does not land on the table before that head contact, AND
  - arm never touches the red ball, AND
  - black distractor (if present) does not hit the bat head, AND
  - bat remains held in the air.
