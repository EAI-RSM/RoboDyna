cook_meat_timer — expert controller demos

Same layout / options as cook_meat, plus a per-station pie timer
(green → yellow → red) that fills with cooking.

default  : cook_button_enabled=false, dual_setup_enabled=false
           single station; contact cook on pan (no key); pie timer runs on contact
opt1     : cook_button_enabled=true,  dual_setup_enabled=false
           single station; hold red cook key; pie timer runs only while held
opt2     : cook_button_enabled=false, dual_setup_enabled=true
           dual stations; both arms; contact cook; one pie timer per station
opt1+2   : cook_button_enabled=true,  dual_setup_enabled=true
           dual stations; each side has its own cook key and pie timer

Success: every station steak returned to its cutting board off the pan with
         grasp_doneness inside target_doneness_range (inclusive).
