🦕 RoboDyna

A benchmark for generalizable dynamic dual-arm manipulation — tasks where the robot must time its actions to a scene that keeps moving and changing. Forked & extended from DOMINO (RoboTwin 2.0 + SAPIEN).

SAPIEN 3.0.3 Embodiment dual UR5 + WSG Export HDF5 + LeRobot v2.1 Platform GB10 / aarch64

What makes it dynamic

Static manipulation benchmarks observe a frozen scene. RoboDyna's tasks evolve while the robot acts — objects stream, fall, ripen, and sway — so success depends on timing and prediction, not just geometry.

⏱️ Time-evolving state

Steaks cook, apples ripen, balls fall — rendered object state advances every physics step, and the reward depends on acting at the right moment.

🤖 Policy-evaluable

Scene dynamics run autonomously and task effects are driven by physical contact (e.g. a gripper pressing a diverter button), so a learned policy is genuinely tested, not just replayed.

🎲 Heavily randomized

Per-episode randomization of counts, speeds, ripen rates, layouts, colours and object variants — for generalization, not memorization.

Featured tasks

Built end-to-end on the dual-UR5 setup. ✅ = 100-episode production dataset collected.

cook_meatdone

Grasp a raw steak, cook it on the pan to a randomized target doneness (the colour browns over time), then lift it off at the right moment.

catch_ramp_balldone

A ball rolls down a ramp and off the front edge; the arm predicts the landing point and pre-positions a cup to catch it.

sort_apples_beltdone

Red/green apples stream down a conveyor; press the matching side button to aim a pivoting diverter blade that routes each into its colour-matched bowl. The button physically drives the diverter.

pick_ripe_appledone

Two apples hang on a tree: one ripens green→red→black (pick it); the other green→yellow→black (ignore). The basket slides left↔right under the branches; the matching arm drops the red apple in.

Prototype tasks

Built and runnable; tuning / validation in progress. Clips are partial.

hit_target

A moving target sways across the table; stick the dart tip in the yellow center (optional moving blocker).

place_block_belt

Set a tall, top-heavy block onto a moving conveyor so it rides upright into a bowl at the exit.

drop_ball_hole

Drop three prisms into their matching holes on a continuously rotating sorter cap.

whack_moles

Strike popping moles with the flat face of a vertically aligned mallet head; touching a rabbit distractor fails.

catch_valley_ball

Push an open box beyond the line to catch a red ball leaving a curved ramp.

catch_cuboid

Grasp the cuboid (or both cuboids) during timed pop-up windows from a hole board; optional opaque lattice.

catch_marbles_trapdoors

Time button presses to drop marbles through trapdoors as they pass.

put_cup_belt

Carry a cup through a swaying curtain of strips and into a slot.

punch_dual_holes

Both arms press buttons to hole-punch files on two belts (default: discrete stop under stamps).

assemble_markers_cylinder

Attach four markers evenly (90° apart) around a vertical cylinder.

control_quality

Stamp red/green tiles that stop under a gantry (max 2s). Default alternating; Opt1 random colors; Opt2 black distractors. Missed tile fails.

save_goal

Place a square blocker in the green zone before the red deadline line to keep a moving ball out of the goal.

dispense_gummy

Press dispense and belt keys so only the target-colored gummies land in the bowl.

play_billiard

Strike the red primary ball into a pocket with a cue stick.

pack_fruits

Pack apples and oranges from two belts into color-matched baskets.

cook_food_timer

Cook food on the KitchenS stove while a pie timer advances only while the burner is on, then plate it.

Simulator: SAPIEN 3.0.3

Standardized for both data collection and evaluation (mixing versions shifts the render & physics defaults). The only release with wheels for both x86_64 and aarch64.

# x86_64 (e.g. RTX 5080)
pip install sapien==3.0.3

# aarch64 (NVIDIA GB10 / Jetson) — official arm64 wheel
pip install https://github.com/haosulab/SAPIEN/releases/download/3.0.3/sapien-3.0.3-cp310-cp310-linux_aarch64.whl

# collect a task
export VK_ICD_FILENAMES=/usr/share/vulkan/icd.d/nvidia_icd.json; unset DISPLAY
bash scripts/collect_data.sh <task> demo_dynamic 0

Authoring a new task touches only a few files and plugs into the shared config; a bundled Claude Code skill (.claude/skills/SAPIEN-task-creator) automates the process. See the repo README for details.
